This research aims to model the unknown link between Coenzyme A (CoA) biosynthesis deficiency and increased arrhythmia risk, which is observed in patients with mutations like PPCS that can lead to dilated cardiomyopathy. The objectives are to develop a detailed model of cardiomyocyte electrophysiology incorporating CoA synthesis and energetics, determine how CoA deficiency modifies the cardiac action potential, and use 3D patient-specific simulations to investigate the combined effects of subcellular changes and geometric abnormalities on arrhythmia generation.

Read more about the project [here](https://uit.no/research/hubmol).

ProCardio Center for innovation, led by Professor Kristina Haugaa, is a research-based innovation center focused on advancing the field of cardiovascular medicine. Our goal is to develop, test, and quality-assure tools that can accurately predict disease progression in individual patients, offering insights into potential future treatments and allowing for optimal intervention to prevent further disease development.

Read more about the project [here](https://www.oslo-universitetssykehus.no/en/avdelinger/hjerte-lunge-og-karklinikken/procardio-center-for-innovation2/).
