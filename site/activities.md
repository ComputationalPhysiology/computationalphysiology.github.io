We recently participated in the [Cardiac Mechanics Benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2024.117485) where different research groups compared their cardiac mechanics solvers on a set of benchmark problems. We implemented the benchmark problems using our FEniCS and the source code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/finsberg/cardiac_benchmark). We have also implemented the benchmark problems in our FEniCSx-based cardiac mechanics solver `fenicsx-pulse`. You can find the implementation of the benchmark problem in `fenicsx-pulse` for the LV [here](https://finsberg.github.io/fenicsx-pulse/demo/time_dependent_bestel_lv.html) and for the BiV here [here](https://finsberg.github.io/fenicsx-pulse/demo/time_dependent_bestel_biv.html).
